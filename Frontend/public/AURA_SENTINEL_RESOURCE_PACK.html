2. AURA Core Pipeline & Architecture

The 9-stage autonomous pipeline linking raw telemetry to board-level investments:

  • 1. System Telemetry Ingest [psutil + OS Sockets]: Continuous streaming of CPU, RAM, disk, and listening sockets.
  • 2. Collect & Normalize [FastAPI + Pydantic]: Multi-source schema validation and timestamp normalization.
  • 3. Threat / Signal Analysis [MITRE ATT&CK]: Maps anomalous activity to adversary tactics (T1046, T1021).
  • 4. Risk Engine [Statistical Scorer]: Composite technical cyber severity scoring (0–100 scale).
  • 5. Financial Exposure [Actuarial Loss Model]: Translates CVSS risk to rupee exposure ranges (₹8L–₹15L prototype).
  • 6. What-If Simulation [Sensitivity Analyzer]: Dynamic wargame testing of open ports, threat signals, and load deltas.
  • 7. 0/1 Knapsack DP Optimization [Dynamic Programming]: Solves discrete budget allocation maximizing security value.
  • 8. AI Contextual Explanation [Groq Cloud LPU]: Natural language summaries and executive defense justification.
  • 9. Blockchain Audit Ledger [SHA-256 + Merkle]: Immutable cryptographic sealing and parent-hash linking.
